✉️ Scheduling

Please schedule by text – to 978.505.5275 – or email – to mick@mickverran.com.

We'll keep in touch, and if 2 days before tour day the forecast shows a high chance of rain, we'll reschedule – our tours are supposed to be fun, not an endurance test.

With that in mind, I'll ask you to pay by Venmo or cash when our tour actually sets off.

Stuff happens; if you need to cancel, please send me a text message. If you cancel within 24 hours of the start of the tour, Venmo-ing me half the fee would be appreciated.
