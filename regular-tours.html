REGULAR TOURS

Iconic places. Untold stories. Unforgettable experiences.

FROM BEACONS & CROOKS TO DEACONS & BOOKS

“That's all I claim for Boston – that it is the thinking center of the continent, and therefore of the planet.” — Oliver Wendell Holmes Snr

A journey across the Shawmut peninsula to the made land of Boston's Back Bay. including 100 anecdotes and 1,000,000 trees.

  • Starts at the State House
  • Ends near Back Bay & Copley T stations
  • Available when the Boston Public Library is open (limited to 12 guests)
  • Allow an extra hour if you haven't visited the Library.

PIZZA, PASTA, PASTRIES, PEOPLE

“The North End smells like garlic, history, and happiness.” — Unknown

Arrive hungry for our tour, which is split into 3 parts – food – walking off the food – then food.

  • Unique history of Boston's "Little Italy"
  • 15+ opportunities to buy food
  • Bring cash (many places don't take cards)
  • Start/finish near the Tony DeMarco statue on Hanover Street
  • Restricted to 6 guests
  • Kids welcome (young ones often lose interest after pizza, canoli, and North End Bingo).

WELL, THAT'S NOT VERY NICE

“That's where you'll find me Along with lovers, muggers, and thieves Oh, but they're cool people.” — The Standells

Explore the darker side of Boston's history on a tour of the beautiful Beacon Hill neighborhood.

  • Persecution, slavery, wars, riots, massacres, bombings, terrorism, robberies, corruption, gangland killings, bizarre murders, and serial killers
  • Focus on how our responses are shaped by our upbringing and the times we live in
  • Lower age limit at your discretion
  • Meet at State House; end at Charles/MGH T
  • Open to 12 guests.

YOU SAY YOU WANT A REVOLUTION

We all want to change our world. So what does the course of the American Revolution in Boston have to teach us about how to do that - collectively and individually?

What would it take for the people of Boston to rise up - again - and restore the possibilities and promise of American Independence? By the end of this tour, you'll have your own revolutionary plan.

  • 3 tours, or combine all 3 in one grand tour
  • Tour 1: Boston Common -> Faneuil Hall
  • Tour 2: North End (start/finish at Faneuil Hall)
  • Tour 3: Across the Charles to Charlestown
  • Tour 4: All 3 combined (lunch & a libation)
